* The BibTeX files served by Scholar live on scholar.googleusercontent.com,
  which this machine's network cannot reach (TLS handshake blocked). The .bib
  files stored here were therefore fetched by DOI content negotiation from
  Crossref (curl -LH "Accept: application/x-bibtex" https://doi.org/<DOI>),
  i.e. the publisher-deposited records — same convention as the 2026-10-09
  reference-insertion batch (.bib + .crossref.json pairs).
* Crossref API JSON for both DOIs is archived alongside (zhang2025_bbae531.
  crossref.json, zhang2026_ady9955.crossref.json). Author lists, titles,
  volumes, issues, article numbers and years were checked against the
  user-supplied citations and match exactly (9 and 11 authors respectively;
  both >5 authors, hence Nature style "Zhang, X. et al.").
